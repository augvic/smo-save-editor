# 📖 Story flags

For the parts of the story that are not tied to one kingdom. Needs a save slot.

- **Bosses**: the fights are numbered in the save's order, because the game's own names for them are not in its data. On means it was seen or won: gatekeeper fights won, first-fight intro shown, rematch intro shown.
- **Cutscenes of the whole game**: the Bowser rematch ending, the Moon basement collapse, the warp hole cutscene, and the first Moon Rock opening.
- **Shop markers**: the shop shows a "NEW" marker on items you have not looked at. **Clear all NEW markers** removes every one.

The cutscenes of a single kingdom are in its page under [Kingdoms](kingdoms.md).
