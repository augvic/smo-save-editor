# 🛡️ Checks and 📋 Report

## 🛡️ Checks
Goes through the open save and lists what does not agree with the game's own rules. **Nothing is changed until you press a fix.**
A red dot on the tab shows when there is something to look at; with nothing wrong it says "Nothing to report".

What it looks for:
- A moon collected in a scenario it is not out in yet.
- A kingdom's **Moon total**, **Moons bought in shop** or **Purple coins spent** that does not follow what is collected or owned.
- A kingdom that was left but whose Moon total is below the Odyssey's requirement.
- **Mario in a locked kingdom** (fix: move Mario to the last unlocked kingdom).
- The **Odyssey level**, and a **first visit** flag that does not follow Main scenario.
- **Camera cutscene flags** that do not follow Main scenario (a kingdom not reached has none played; a reached one has played its intro).
- A **locked kingdom** with collected moons or purple coins. You choose: unlock it, or clear its moons and coins.
- A "Gather" Power Moon achievement marked while the total is below its goal.

Each line has its own button. Lines with **several buttons** are your decision. **Fix all (n)** applies the safe, single fixes at once; it leaves those with a choice.

The editor does not flag what the game's own saves do (for example a story step that does not match its scenario), so a clean list is not proof that the game will accept the file.

## 📋 Report
Everything the editor knows about that the save does not have yet, for the kingdoms that are unlocked: per kingdom, the missing Power Moons, purple coins, shop items, checkpoints and side areas; then, for the whole game, the missing captures, music and achievements. **Copy** puts the text on the clipboard, to paste into notes.
